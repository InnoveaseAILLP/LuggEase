Privacy Policy

Updated 16 November 2026

LuggEase is operated by Innovease AI & Technology Transformation Consulting LLP. We process your sign-in details, contact information, booking addresses and order details to provide moving services and support. Firebase Authentication handles account sign-in; authorized service partners receive the information needed to fulfil their assigned booking.

When you type into pickup or drop address search, your search text and a temporary search-session identifier are sent through our server to Google Maps Platform to return address suggestions. Selecting a suggestion requests its full address. We do not store suggestion lists. You can enter an address manually instead. Your selected booking address is retained with your order for fulfilment and invoicing.

For an assigned delivery, the partner may explicitly share precise device location while the tracking screen remains open. Location reports and their accuracy are visible only to the booking customer, assigned partner and authorized administrators. Sharing stops when the app is backgrounded or the tracking screen closes. Routine location reports expire after 30 days; evidence attached to a billing dispute or accepted charge is retained with the transaction record. A reported path is not independent proof of congestion.

We retain the customer ownership declaration and two handwritten pickup signatures with the transfer agreement and invoice. At the destination, the recipient name, handwritten delivery signature and delivery acknowledgements are retained with the order and final invoice. Customer star ratings and optional feedback comments are stored to assess delivery service. Signatures are private and appear only in authorized document downloads. Aadhaar numbers and Aadhaar OTPs are not requested. Payment references and instalment amounts are recorded; card and bank credentials are handled by Razorpay checkout, not stored by LuggEase.

Authorized administrators can view account roles, recorded transactions and operational reports, including aggregated city/state order demand and sales figures. These reports are not available to customer or delivery-partner accounts.

Google processes Maps usage under its Privacy Policy. Account and transaction records are retained as needed for service, legal and accounting obligations. To request account information, correction or deletion, contact support@innovease-ai.in.
